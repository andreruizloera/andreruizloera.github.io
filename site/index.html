About this site

This is the canonical home for Andre Ruiz Loera — a single owned place that represents the work, the reading, and the thinking. It is a record and a workshop, not a brochure.

Rather than asserting depth, it tries to show it: through reading notes written out in full, lists curated across a dozen domains, and essays that earn their place. No framework, no tracking, no build step — plain pages that read cleanly on any screen and are built to last.

Sections

What you'll find here

Five surfaces in one consistent voice. Some are richly populated; others are growing as real items land.

  1. 01 Blog Fewer, better essays on mathematics, markets, and craft. Each post earns its place; quality over cadence.
  2. 02 Annotations The intellectual core: structured reading notes across books, math, finance, and philosophy, with real mathematics rendered properly.
  3. 03 Reading Lists Curated booklists across twelve domains — classics, finance, mathematics, economics, and more. A navigable map of the work.
  4. 04 Research Papers and projects, listed as they exist rather than padded — a credibility surface kept honest.
  5. 05 Press Media and mentions, gathered in one place. Filled as real items arrive.

Currently

The structure and design are settled; substance is the work. Three things are being actively grown.

  • Seeding the annotations

    Publishing the reading notes whose sources already exist, then expanding across books, math, finance, and philosophy.

  • Connecting the lists

    Linking reading-list entries to their annotations as they are written, turning long lists into evidence of follow-through.

  • Writing that compounds

    A growing archive of essays and notes — owned outright, built to outlast any single platform.
