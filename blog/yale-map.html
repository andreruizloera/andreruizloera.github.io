The Major Map

August 24, 2026 · 39 courses · 6 majors

What the 39 free Yale courses add up to, and what each block is for

Read as a booklist, Open Yale Courses is 328 texts in no particular order. Read as a curriculum, it clusters into six majors — and the clusters overlap, because Ethics, Politics & Economics is built out of the other three rather than sitting beside them. This is that structure.

    Rows are courses, columns are majors. A course with two marks counts twice, which is the whole point of EP&E. The thirteen below the rule count toward none of the six.

    On the mapping. Course titles, text counts and links come straight from the syllabi. The course-to-major assignment is my own, made from department codes and the way Yale's majors are normally built; cross-listings are the sort of thing a director of undergraduate studies rules on, so read the columns as an argument rather than a transcript.

    On the rationale. Also mine, read off what each block teaches rather than off any official description.
