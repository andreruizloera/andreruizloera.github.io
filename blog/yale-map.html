What Yale's 39 free courses actually add up to

Yale has 39 courses online with full syllabi and no paywall. I went through all of them to work out what they add up to, and the answer is pieces of six majors with a lot of overlap, because Ethics, Politics & Economics borrows most of its courses from Economics and Philosophy. This is the whole thing on one page with what I think each block is worth.
