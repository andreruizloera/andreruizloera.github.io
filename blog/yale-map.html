What the 39 free Yale courses add up to

Yale puts 39 courses online with full syllabi. Between them they cover chunks of six majors. The chunks overlap heavily, because Ethics, Politics & Economics borrows most of its courses from Economics and Philosophy. Here is the whole thing in one place, with what each block actually gets you.
